PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chaosverse website (“the Site”) handles information when you visit it. The Site is intended as an informational landing page for the Chaosverse YouTube channel.

1. Information we collect

The Site does not currently provide user accounts, forms, comments, or direct payment functionality. We do not intentionally ask you to provide personal information, and this static website does not itself operate a visitor-tracking database.

2. Hosting and server logs

The Site may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to provide, secure, and maintain the hosting service. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

3. External links and platforms

This Site links to YouTube and may link to other third-party websites. When you follow those links, the third party’s privacy policy and practices apply. We do not control how third-party platforms collect or use information. Please review Google’s Privacy Policy before using YouTube.

4. Cookies and analytics

The Site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies and does not currently use third-party analytics. Third-party services you visit may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

5. Children’s privacy

The Site is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Site operator, please contact the operator so the issue can be reviewed.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Site or its features change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method listed on the Chaosverse YouTube channel, if available.

This is a general-purpose starter policy for the current static site, not legal advice. Update it if you add analytics, embedded media, forms, mailing lists, advertising, or other data collection.
